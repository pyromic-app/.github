<h1 align="center">Pyromic</h1>

<p align="center">
  Your files, handled automatically.<br>
  Pyromic watches your folders and runs every new file through a flow you design: video, music, subtitles, documents, archives or any other file.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://pyromic.com">pyromic.com</a>

## How it works

1. **Point Pyromic at a folder.** It becomes a library, and Pyromic picks up every file that arrives.
2. **Draw a flow.** Connect nodes on a canvas: each one does one job, and conditions decide which way a file goes.
3. **Let it run.** Files go through the flow on their own, on your server or spread across other machines you add.

## What a flow can do

**Video**
- Convert to modern formats such as HEVC and AV1, and many more, on your graphics card or processor
- Aim for a quality target and let Pyromic find the smallest file that meets it
- Keep HDR, crop black bars, resize, clean up, sharpen, deinterlace, change frame rate and more
- Choose, sort and rename audio and subtitle tracks; convert audio; even out loudness
- Repackage without re-encoding, check a file plays end to end, make thumbnails

**Music**
- Convert between audio formats
- Look up every song and fix its tags, album by album
- Add cover art and lyrics
- Turn a song into a video

**Subtitles and speech**
- Transcribe speech into subtitles or text, on your own hardware
- Turn picture subtitles into text
- Translate subtitles, detect languages, burn subtitles into the picture

**Documents, archives and everyday files**
- Convert documents between formats
- Unpack and make archives
- Rename, copy, move, delete and date files; create folders; write text and checksum files

**Your other apps**
- Look up films, shows and anime and name them properly
- Write the information and artwork your media server reads, and tell it to refresh
- Send notifications to your favourite chat and alert services

## Built for your own machines

- **Runs where you do:** Windows, macOS, Linux, Docker and Unraid.
- **Use every machine you have:** add runners on other computers and Pyromic shares the work, using each one's graphics card.
- **Managed from your browser:** the server runs in the background; you use it from any device on your network.
- **Your files stay with you:** all the processing happens on your hardware. Pyromic only goes online when a flow asks it to, such as looking up a film or an album.

## Status

Pyromic is in development and has not been released yet. The first release will be announced on [pyromic.com](https://pyromic.com).
